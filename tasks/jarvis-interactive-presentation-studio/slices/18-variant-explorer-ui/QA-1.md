## 4. Findings

### BLOCKING

**B1 - A deep branch makes the tree unreadable (also at 1280, worst at 800).** `jarvis/runtime/control_center_presentation_studio_explorer_core.js:35-36` (`MAX_DEPTH_SHOWN=10`, `INDENT_PX=14`) + `..._widgets.js:80-85` (rail kept at 140 px, plus a `↳NN` chip and the twist). Scenario: chain of 31 + 9 leaves at its end, depth 15 hostile nodes. At 1280x720 every row deeper than 10 gets a title area of about 100 px: "Fin de chaine..." x 9 identical, "<img src=x o...", "javascript:ale..."; the lineage lines vanish after depth 10 so the branch can no longer be followed. At 800x600 the title column is a single letter ("C...", "F...", "F...") and the rows are indistinguishable except by `#number`. Evidence: `scratchpad/qa18/out/s2_1280x720_deep31.png`, `s2_800x600_deep31.png`, `s2_1920x1080_deep31.png` (readable only at 1920). The Slice goal is a tree that "is not unreadable for realistic branch counts" and the acceptance criterion is choosing a branch visually; a chain of 12-15 successive refinements is exactly the normal voice-driven case (every "make it more sober" branches from the previous one), and the shipped 64-variant browser test never goes deeper than 3 levels. The implementer's test titled "nothing clipped at three sizes" runs the 12-variant story only. Fix (cheap): indent step shrinking after level 4 (14 -> 6 px) and cap at about 6 visible levels, put the depth chip in the meta line instead of before the title, let the title wrap on 2 lines (row is 48 px: title 2 lines, meta in the tooltip) or make the left pane 320 px minimum / resizable, and add a deep-chain (depth 20) case to the 64-variant browser test asserting the title column is at least about 120 px at 1280 and 800.

### POLISH (should be done, not merge-blocking alone)

- **P1 - A fixed-height squeeze when the archive section is open.** `x_128_archive_open.png`: at 1280x720 with Archivées open the live tree shows 5 of 64 rows and the archive 5 of 128. Give the open archive its own panel (overlay or tabs: Vivantes | Archivées) or a 60/40 split with scroll, and persist the choice (it is already in localStorage).
- **P2 - The Slice 03 fullscreen prompt covers the top of the preview and half of the mode chip** ("Plein écran en attente de votre clic" is cut under it at 1280; at 800 it hides the title, the `ACTIF` flag and part of row #1) for up to 30 s, which is exactly the voice-open path. Dock the prompt to the header strip of the explorer or shorten its text in this host (e.g. `compact` option) so the stage and chip stay readable.
- **P3 - Duplicate message under the archive dialog.** `c_stale.png`: the floating notice ("Ce qui serait archivé a changé...") is drawn clipped under the scrim and repeated by the dialog warn box. Suppress the notice when a dialog shows the same sentence.
- **P4 - A failure notice does not "stay until dismissed" in all cases.** `explorer.js:105-110` `say()` replaces `S.notice` unconditionally: a later `say('info',...)` (graph changed elsewhere at the 10 s poll, "Une opération est déjà en cours", a click on a disabled action) overwrites an undismissed failure. Code evidence only, not reproduced. Keep a failed/stale notice unless the new one is also failed, or queue it.
- **P5 - Voice switch loses a typed form.** `open()` for another presentation while a branch/rename dialog is open closes it with `closeDialog(false)` and `quiet` (`explorer.js:1093`), silently dropping the user's text. Refuse, or ask, when `S.dialog` is open.
- **P6 - Full title not available on narrow rows.** Titles are cut with an ellipsis and the row tooltip carries only the rationale (`widgets.js:78`). Put the full title first in `title`, then the rationale.
- **P7 - 11.5 px keyboard hint (`core.js:450`, also 11.5 px scene role and menu keys).** It is the only visible place that explains N / F2 / A / Suppr, below the 12 px floor, and reads as noise. Better: show the keys as `kbd` badges inside the action buttons (the buttons already have `aria-keyshortcuts`), keep a 13 px help line only on the empty/first-open state. At 800 the hint wraps to two lines and eats tree height.
- **P8 - No type-ahead in the tree** (APG optional). With 64 numbered variants, typing `4`,`2` to reach #42 is the expected power-user move; low cost since numbers are immutable.
- **P9 - Duplicate screen-reader announcement.** The treeitem carries a full `aria-label` and a nested `button` twist with its own `aria-expanded`/label (`widgets.js:86-91`); mark the twist `aria-hidden` (it is not in the tab order) or drop its `aria-expanded`. Needs the Human NVDA check to confirm.
- **P10 - Archived rows all read "sous-branche d'une variante vivante"** (`widgets.js:100`). Correct but repeated 126 times in my 128 case; say it once as a header of the section and show "archivée il y a ..." per row.
- **P11 - Test stability (F3 below).** Counted here too because the 25 s real-Chrome tests are the only proof of several guards (see M4).

### ISSUE (not defects of this Slice, or accepted limits to track)

- **F3 - Flaky browser test (about 2 failures in 26 runs of `test_activate_branch_rename...`)**: a failed GET `.../variants/psv_...` lands in the page console, caught by `noise()`. Not reproduced under my isolated loop (8/8) nor in 6 targeted archive-then-select runs, so the cause is a hypothesis: `schedulePreview`'s armed timer (`explorer.js:528-534`) skips reads only while `S.busy` is a write; `perform()`'s `finally{endBusy()}` runs before `after()` re-reads the graph, so a 120 ms timer can fire in that gap and ask for the variant that was just archived (the page then recovers with `preview_variant_gone`, so users see nothing; only the console 404 and the test). Cheap fix: `cancelLater(previewTimer)` at the start of every non-plan `perform` and let `after()` reschedule; or whitelist that known 404 in `noise()` with an assertion on the recovery log.
- **F4 - Graphical entry is not delivered in Slice 18.** The only entries today are the command channel (PowerShell/agent, no `open_explorer` tool until Slice 21) and the JS console. I agree with the implementer's reasoning for not building the "Variantes..." stage-window entry (that window exists only while a run plays and the explorer is refused then, so the entry would always answer a refusal) and with the PM plan: a call to `JarvisStudioExplorer.open()` from a button in the Slice 07 inspector and from the stopped-run band, added at merge time. Conditions I recommend: (a) the merge commit adds both buttons with a browser test clicking them (a real click, so fullscreen enters at once) and a test that the button is absent/disabled while a run plays; (b) the PM tracks it as an explicit acceptance line, because until then a non-technical user has no way to reach this feature, so "entry from voice and graphical affordances" is only half proven at this commit; (c) the stopped-run band lives in the player module of Slice 12, not in Slice 07, so name its owner.
- **F5 - The state mirror is writable by any local process.** `POST /api/presentation-studio/explorer/state` without an `Origin` header (curl, a script) was accepted: I made the mirror say `open / fullscreen / entered` for a presentation while no page existed (it stays so up to 60 s, then `unknown`). Same trust model as the fullscreen channel (loopback processes are trusted; browsers and null-origin frames are blocked, I verified all four), and the agent contract says "read the mirror, never assume", so the claim "never claims fullscreen before the browser says so" holds for the open receipt and for a real page, but the mirror is only as honest as its writers. Document it; for the stronger claim, have the agent re-ask the page (a `state` command through the same channel) rather than trust the mirror for anything that gets spoken. Same family: any local process can `close` the user's explorer or re-arm the fullscreen prompt every second with `open` (no rate limit); tolerable, note for Slice 21.
- **F6 - Receipt deadline vs. page work.** `DELIVERY_DEADLINE_S = 4`, but the page does `corePlaying()` (up to 4 s timeout) then `loadGraph` (up to 10 s) then up to 2.5 s of fullscreen patience before answering `open`. Normal case 0.2-0.7 s (measured), but a slow Core makes the agent get `504 explorer_command_expired` ("outcome unknown") while the explorer does open. Return the receipt as soon as the shell is drawn (the domain docstring even says "the graph load is not awaited", the code does await it), or raise the deadline to 8-10 s.
- **F7 - `window.JarvisStudioExplorer.instance` publicly exposes** `handleCommand`, `runAction`, `ui()` (frozen only shallowly). Same-origin page code only (prefab frames are sandboxed), but it is an unneeded surface; hide it behind a test-only flag.
- **F8 - Mirror claims `open` for up to 60 s after the page died** (seen when a Chrome was killed: the next run's first `asked.snapshot` still said `open`). Intended and dated (`reported_s_ago`), fine; make the agent-facing text say "last reported N s ago".
- **F9 - Module size.** Controller 1301 lines (three modules 2442 in all). Within what the repo does elsewhere (player 611, reload 477 are smaller, but no documented cap found). The controller still mixes network, selection, dialogs, fullscreen, commands and reporting; the preview/art-direction block (about 130 lines) and the fullscreen/arm block (about 80) split out cleanly into a fourth file if the PM wants it. Not a blocker.

Acceptance criterion ("understand how the project evolved and choose a branch visually without reading raw metadata"): **met for shallow or wide trees** (the 12-variant tree reads at a glance: lineage rails, ACTIF flag, live preview, palette chip, calm hierarchy, honest empty/stale/limit states), **not met for deep chains** (B1) and only weakly for "visually" (no per-variant visual identity in the tree).

Design critique (impeccable lens): strong hierarchy (title / tree / preview / actions in one glance), restrained palette, consistent chips, the archive dialog is excellent (exact list with short ids, red confirm last, `Annuler` first, live countdown, stale re-plan wording). Weak points: density of the left pane once archive is open (P1), the 11.5 px grey text on dark for secondary info, the redundant chip row ("Sans partition" amber warning on every variant reads as an error when it is a normal state; make absent score neutral or hide it unless a score is expected), and no empty/loading state screenshots were verified by me (I saw the loading veil only in code).
